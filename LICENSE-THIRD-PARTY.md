## Ported code

### BelotGameEngine (MIT)

`engines/belot.lua` is a Lua port (a derived work) of the game rules of
[NikolayIT/BelotGameEngine](https://github.com/NikolayIT/BelotGameEngine), `src/Belot.Engine/`
at commit `a4a6e4d9d27ce5cec42ee5648b252452d07f1aac` (package version 2.0.0, retrieved 2026-11-21):
the auction, the legal cards, the trick winner, the announces and the scoring. No C# source is
shipped with the addon. `tests/fixtures/belot/*.lua` are records of matches played on that engine,
generated by `tools/belot-parity/` (which references the engine as a build-time dependency; its
sources are not in this repository).

## Sounds

The games' sounds in `media/sounds/`: Texas Hold'em's (ui/games/HoldemSounds.lua) and every other game's
(ui/games/GameSounds.lua). All but one are made from five packs by Kenney, https://kenney.nl: "Casino Audio",
"Interface Sounds", "Impact Sounds", "RPG Audio" and "Music Jingles", all [Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(public domain; credit is not required, it is given here). Each was trimmed of its leading and trailing silence, made
mono at 44.1 kHz, normalized and saved as Ogg Vorbis by `tools/sounds/make_sounds.py`; the two marked "mixed" are two
source files mixed into one, the second starting later.
