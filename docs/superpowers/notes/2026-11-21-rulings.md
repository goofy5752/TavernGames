## Accepted residuals (to revisit in the stakes stage)

Stages D and E play for chips and fun only. With real stakes each of these needs its own ruling. Sources: the D and E ledgers and Addendum 6/7 of `docs/superpowers/notes/engine-contract.md`.

Stage D, Tabla dice (Addendum 6; rulings D8, D9, D13 and the Task 3 residual):
1. F8, forged-move check: in an open game a modified host can move for the guest and the guest sees it on its own board (as in Морски шах). When a client is behind by events, the moves in between cannot be checked.
2. F9, host reload/silence: a host that reloads or goes silent after it has seen the guest's number re-rolls one throw (no kept seed for the opener, no DealKeep for dice). It costs the host role and about 75 s, so a host gains one re-roll per hosting.
3. A throw made alone proves only against the opener's own commit: a host that keeps its open from the guest and throws alone chooses those dice. The guest's client counts it (unheard, red from the second in a match; a dodge when pushed).
4. The guest cannot pick its number, and a modified host client could look at the guest's seed before publishing; it cannot change its own seed because its commit is out first (stated in `help.fair.dice`).
5. After a /reload or crash a client holds only its kept seed: a valid alone proof, its roll counts, game index and cascade number are taken as they fit each other. A hidden alone throw I never saw is free once per match (unheard, red from the second).
6. A loading screen excuses one throw (shown amber; resync window 45 s = DICE_WAIT + PING_EVERY + SYN_MIN_GAP), so one throw per loading screen may go uncounted. A cascade number I did not see played costs one unheard.
7. A watcher that misses every message of an intermediate host and then sees the old host again under a higher epoch cannot judge the gap (Addendum 6).

Stage E, Hold'em sealed deal (Addendum 7 "Residuals, to revisit in the stakes stage"):
1. Host-initiated stands cannot be verified by members: a stand the host plays for a seat is a trusted claim (the Presence check was dropped as a framing risk).
2. A host that holds a step past FAIR_WAIT and then voids is not flagged (only a void earlier than the wait is).
3. A named seat is held out only by the host that named it (a new host does not know the record), and a re-sit escapes the hold because it is keyed by seat; the 3-seat release order is also a known edge.
4. A seed lapse that a lying dealer provokes (a deck it already knows) shows only as an amber lapse count.
5. A member accepts an away of its own seat after 13 s in stage `commit` (FAIR_WAIT − AUTO_GRACE slack, host may act 7 s early unflagged); a seed-stage away of any seat after FAIR_WAIT is not flagged. Own-seat offline stands cannot be flagged by the seat itself (others and watchers do).
6. A host can withhold one player's key, so that hand stays open (gold/amber) and an innocent player is named as the one who left no key.
7. A loading screen excuses one dice throw (the stage D item above; cross-reference only).
8. A modified host can still draw a member's key while its hand is live (rule 6a narrows this, it cannot close it): (a) by events — forged events end the hand and the game, the member sends its key, and the member flags it red only afterwards via the snapshot; (b) after the member's own /reload mid-hand the first snapshot is all it has and one saying the hand and game ended is believed. One hand per victim per game; a hand of more players needs every other participant's key by the same trick. An honest member that heals by snapshot keeps its key and shows a red self-line (accepted per ruling). A full cure needs a protocol change (for example members' keys released only against the other participants' signed word that the hand ended).
9. The dealer who stands up mid-hand voids the hand and leaves with its chips (the dealer's stand-void refund, ruling E15), stacks being free; a player vanishing without a key leaves its hands unverified (amber, named, not red); a hand is readable once all its players have left; a player who stands up gets no verdict unless it stays to watch.
10. Hold'em is capped at 6 seats (ruling E1) until a later message-budget stage; the measured budget (ruling Task 5) means a slower table on bad links.
