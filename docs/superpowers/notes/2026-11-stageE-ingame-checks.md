# Stage E: in-game checks (Тексас Холдем)

Run these in the game client on Forever with `/console scriptErrors 1` on throughout.
Nothing here is marked passed on the strength of the unit tests: every row stays `not yet measured` until the result is written in by hand.
Rows 1-9 need one character (solo with bots). Rows 10-19 and 22-24 need two or three characters in different zones (rows 22 and 24 are easier with a third as a watcher). Row 20 needs as many guild members as can be found. Row 21 needs one character.

Scope of this stage: Холдем (`Games.PLAYABLE` gains `holdem`) with play chips only (no gold, no rebuy), a **six-seat** table with **one watcher** at most (nine seats wait for a later message-budget stage), the sealed deal (one table key per sitting, the dealer deals every street, the book is checked at the close), No Limit and Pot Limit. Table frame 760 x 532 (felt 732 x 440).

Geometry as built (it differs from the first plan; Task 9, fix round 1): the note under the board is at TOPLEFT (12, -366), 270 wide, left-justified, in the close button's place (the two are never shown together); row 3 is fold 56 wide at x 476, call 86 at x 536, raise 100 at x 626 (all at y -388, 26 high); a button label wider than the button minus 6 px steps its font down from 13 to 10.

Message budget as measured in the simulator (real wire format, Task 5): an **ordinary hand is 143 whispers (EV 138 + PRV 5) and 22.0-22.5 KB**; a **full ring** (six seats and one watcher, played to the showdown with every seat in every street) is **203 whispers (192 EV, 5 PRV, 6 TCK) and 30.3-31.1 KB**, pinned at 203 and at most 32 500 B. One watcher adds about 4.6 KB a hand, so a full ring without it is about 25.9 KB. Six commits are about 370 bytes packed (about 410 with ChatThrottleLib's 40), still two addon message parts. Row 20 is where this meets the real ChatThrottleLib.
